# YouTube Kanalı için 8 Gemini Promptu


> [!NOTE]
> Kullanım: İlgili prompt'u kopyalayıp Gemini'ye yapıştırın. Köşeli parantez içindeki alanları kendi kanal bilgilerinizle doldurun.
>
> Önerilen sıra: Önce kanal temelini (1–3), ardından ana sayfa ve görsel kimliği (4–5), keşfedilebilirliği (6–7) ve dönüşümü (8) çalışın.
>

### İçindekiler

### 1. The Ultimate YouTube Profile Makeover 🚀

```
Act as an expert YouTube brand strategist and SEO specialist. Audit my channel’s niche, target audience, current About section, playlists, and latest 5 videos. Then create a complete channel makeover plan, including a compelling channel name, keyword-optimized bio, clear brand positioning, eye-catching banner concept, organized playlist structure, featured video strategy, and a professional channel icon brief. Make every element consistent, audience-focused, and designed to improve discoverability, build trust, and turn visitors into subscribers.

My niche: [Insert your niche]
My channel URL: [Insert your channel link]
```

### 2. The YouTube Bio Optimization Lab 🎯

```
Act as a professional YouTube copywriter and audience growth strategist. Create 10 compelling About section variations for my channel. First, identify my niche, target audience, their biggest challenges, the transformation my content offers, and the tone of my best-performing videos. Make each bio engaging, SEO-friendly, and under 1,000 characters, with a strong opening hook, a clear value proposition, relevant credibility signals, a compelling reason to subscribe, and a direct call to action. Then rank the top 3 bios based on clarity, discoverability, audience appeal, and subscriber conversion potential. Explain why each stands out and recommend the strongest option for my channel.

My niche: [Insert your niche]
My channel URL: [Insert your channel link]
```

### 3. The Ultimate YouTube Playlist Blueprint 📚

```
Act as an expert YouTube content strategist and channel growth consultant. Design an optimized playlist system for my channel homepage. Analyze my niche, core audience, main content themes, most popular videos, frequently asked questions, and the biggest concerns new viewers may have. Then create 4–6 strategic playlists, each with an SEO-friendly title, engaging description, thumbnail design concept, recommended videos to include, and a logical viewing sequence. Organize the playlists to guide new visitors from beginner-friendly content to advanced topics, build trust, increase watch time, and encourage viewers to explore more of my channel. Finally, recommend the ideal playlist order for my homepage and explain why it works.

My niche: [Insert your niche]
My channel URL: [Insert your channel link]
```

### 4. The 3-Video Trailer Strategy 🎬

```
Act as a YouTube channel growth strategist. Create a 3-video homepage lineup: 1) Authority Anchor — introduce my mission and expertise, 2) Value Blueprint — deliver my best tutorial, and 3) Lead Magnet Bridge — drive viewers to my product or offer. Write a powerful 15-second opening hook for each video to capture attention and convert viewers into subscribers or customers.

My niche: [Insert niche]
My offer: [Insert product or service]
```

### 5. The Ultimate YouTube Banner Blueprint 🎨

```
Act as a professional visual creative director. Analyze my channel niche and create a detailed Canva/Photoshop banner brief with eye-catching colors, strong contrast, a 3-word value proposition, upload schedule, relevant visual elements, and mobile-safe text alignment. Make the design clean, professional, and instantly recognizable.

My niche: [Insert topic]
```

### 6. The High-CTR Packaging Audit 🚀

```
Act as a YouTube packaging expert. Analyze my last 5 video titles and thumbnail concepts. Score each for curiosity, mobile readability, and SEO potential. Suggest 3 stronger title alternatives (under 6 words) and matching 3-word thumbnail text to boost clicks.

My video titles & thumbnails: [Insert details]
```

### 7. The YouTube Keyword Architecture 🔍

```
Act as a YouTube SEO expert. Generate 15 high-intent channel keywords and relevant tag clusters for my niche. Prioritize search demand, audience relevance, and advertiser-friendly topics with strong monetization potential. Organize them by category and recommend the best keywords to target first.

My niche: [Insert niche]
```

### 8. The Pinned Comment Conversion Funnel 💬

```
Act as a conversion copywriter. Write a compelling 3-line YouTube pinned comment that grabs attention, promotes my free resources, and guides viewers to my store with a clear CTA. Keep it short, engaging, and action-oriented.

My resource/store link: [Insert URL]
```
