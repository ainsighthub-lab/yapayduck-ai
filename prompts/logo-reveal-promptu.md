# AI Brand Logo Reveal Guide


> [!NOTE]
> AI Brand Logo Reveal Guide (Özet / Quick checklist)
>
> - 2 görsel yükle: (1) Storefront final referans + (2) Resmi logo dosyası
> - Detayları ekle: Brand + Famous for
> - 10 sn / 16:9 / tek plan cinematic reveal
> - Final kare storefront referansı ile birebir aynı olmalı

AI Brand Logo Reveal Guide

### Step 1 — Upload Two Images

1. Upload the storefront/final reference image containing the completed logo.
2. Upload a clean image of the official brand logo.

> [!NOTE]
> Critical: The storefront image will be the final frame — not the opening frame.
>

### Step 2 — Add These Details

Brand: [BRAND NAME]  

Famous for: [PRODUCT OR SERVICE]

Example:  

Brand: Benne  

Famous for: Bangalore dosa and butter

### Step 3 — Copy and Paste This Prompt

> [!NOTE]
> PROMPT (Copy/Paste) — 7s / 16:9 / Ultra-realistic / One-shot
>

```
Create an outstanding 7-second ultra-realistic cinematic logo-reveal animation in 16:9.
The first uploaded image defines the exact location, architecture, lighting, camera angle, logo placement and final frame. The second uploaded image defines the exact official logo.
The storefront image is not the opening frame. Begin at the same location with only the logo completely removed. Restore the blank wall or surface naturally behind it. Preserve everything else exactly.
[BRAND NAME] is famous for [PRODUCT OR SERVICE]. Design a spectacular, original reveal using materials, ingredients, craftsmanship or movement directly connected to what the brand is famous for.
Begin the animation immediately. Do not hold on a static blank wall.
During the first second, introduce the empty storefront through a dynamic but controlled camera movement.
From approximately 1–5.5 seconds, the brand-related material enters the scene and transforms into the exact official logo. Make the construction bold, fast, visually satisfying and physically realistic. The logo must become a real dimensional sign with accurate thickness, mounting depth, contact shadows, texture, illumination and environmental reflections.
Do not use a generic fade-in, simple light trace or random particle effect. The reveal must feel unique to the brand.
From 5.5–7 seconds, complete the logo and move smoothly into the exact composition of the uploaded storefront image. Hold the finished logo clearly for the final second.
The final frame must precisely match the uploaded storefront reference. Preserve the official logo’s exact spelling, typography, spacing, colours, proportions and symbols.
One seamless continuous shot. No cuts, transitions, location changes, reverse animation or time jumps.
No incorrect lettering, duplicate logo elements, flat graphic overlay, distorted architecture, new people, random objects, excessive smoke, uncontrolled fire or CGI appearance.
```

> [!NOTE]
> Quality checks (hızlı kontrol):
>
> - Logo birebir aynı: spelling / typography / spacing / colours / proportions / symbols
> - 3D tabelaya dönüşsün: thickness + mounting depth + contact shadows + reflections
> - Yasaklar: fade-in, light trace, random particles, cut/transition, location change, reverse, time jump
